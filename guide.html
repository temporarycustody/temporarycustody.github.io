How to read a study

Each study is read one step at a time, and it is the same in all of them. Nothing moves on until you do, unless you press Play.

Moving on and back

A study opens on its first step. Go on when you have read it; go back whenever you like.

Click→Space
Go on. On a touch screen, tap. Enter and Page Down do the same.
←orPage Up
Go back. On a touch screen, swipe right.
HomeEnd
Jump to the first step or the last.
Parts
Opens a list of the parts of the study, so you can go straight to one. Esc closes it.
The rail
The line of ticks along the bottom is the whole study. Each tick is a step.

Playing it through

If you would rather watch than click, press Play, or P on a keyboard. The study goes on by itself at a reading pace. Press it again to pause where you are.

Sound

The studies carry sound: a quiet bed under each, and sounds for what happens in the text. Your browser only allows sound after you have clicked, so it starts with your first click on the opening screen.

MorSound on
Turns the sound off and on. The button shows the state it is in.

A study can be read in full with the sound off.

Seeing the source

Where a study quotes the work, or names a chapter, you can click the quotation or the chapter to see the passage it comes from. The passage opens over the study. Esc, or a click outside it, closes it and you are back where you were.

If motion or sound is a problem

The loading screens hold still when your device is set to reduce motion. Any loading screen can be skipped with a click, a key or a tap. The studies can be read with the sound off.

Back to the contents at any point with Home, at the top of the loading screen and in each study.
